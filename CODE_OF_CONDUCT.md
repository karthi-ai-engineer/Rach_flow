# Code of conduct

Rflow follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be kind and patient, assume good intent, and keep discussions about the work. Harassment, insults, personal
or political attacks, sexual language or images, and publishing anyone's private information (their voice and their
dictations included) are not accepted, in issues, pull requests, discussions or anywhere you represent the project.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour to the maintainer at **karthi.ai.engineer@gmail.com**. Every report is read and kept
private. The maintainer may remove comments, code or issues, and may block anyone from the project, temporarily or for
good, as the Contributor Covenant's enforcement guidelines describe.
